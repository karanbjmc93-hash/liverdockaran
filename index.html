About Dr. Karan Kumar

Dr. Karan Kumar is a Hepatologist and Liver Transplant Physician with expertise in the diagnosis and management of fatty liver disease, hepatitis, cirrhosis, liver failure, liver cancer and liver transplantation. He completed his DM in Hepatology & Liver Transplantation from the Institute of Liver and Biliary Sciences (ILBS), New Delhi and currently serves as Associate Professor at Mahatma Gandhi Medical College & Hospital, Jaipur.

Liver Myths & Facts

❌ Myth

Fatty liver occurs only in people who drink alcohol.


✅ Fact

Most patients develop fatty liver because of obesity, diabetes and metabolic dysfunction.

❌ Myth

Normal liver tests always mean a healthy liver.


✅ Fact

Significant liver disease may exist despite normal liver enzymes.

❌ Myth

Herbal medicines are always safe.


✅ Fact

Many herbal and alternative medicines can cause serious liver injury.
