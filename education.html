What Is Fatty Liver Disease (MASLD)?

Fatty liver disease occurs when excess fat accumulates in the liver. It is commonly associated with obesity, diabetes, high cholesterol and sedentary lifestyle. If left untreated, fatty liver can progress to liver inflammation, cirrhosis and liver cancer.

Can Fatty Liver Be Reversed?

Yes. Weight reduction, regular exercise, control of diabetes and healthy eating can significantly improve fatty liver and may even reverse early disease.

Early Warning Signs of Cirrhosis

Persistent fatigue, swelling of legs, abdominal distension, jaundice, gastrointestinal bleeding and confusion may indicate advanced liver disease and require specialist evaluation.

Understanding Hepatitis B

Hepatitis B is a viral infection that can cause chronic liver disease. Vaccination is highly effective and treatment is available for eligible patients.

When Is Liver Transplant Needed?

Liver transplantation may be required in patients with advanced cirrhosis, liver failure or selected cases of liver cancer.

Common Myths & Facts

Myth: Fatty liver only affects alcohol users.

Fact: Most Indian patients have metabolic fatty liver disease.

Myth: Normal liver tests always mean a healthy liver.

Fact: Significant liver disease may exist despite normal liver enzymes.

Myth: Herbal medicines are always safe.

Fact: Many herbal products can cause severe liver injury.
